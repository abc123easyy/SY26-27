#4: Sushi

Sushi is one of my favorite foods because it looks colorful and has interesting flavors. I like that there are many different kinds of sushi to try. Some rolls have seafood, while others use vegetables and other ingredients.

Facts About Sushi

  • Sushi is a Japanese food made with specially prepared rice.
  • Some types include raw fish, cooked seafood, or vegetables.
  • Maki sushi is rolled in seaweed.
  • Sushi is often served with soy sauce and pickled ginger.
