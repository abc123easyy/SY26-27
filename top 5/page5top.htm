#5: Ice Cream

Ice cream is one of my favorite desserts because it is sweet and refreshing. I like that there are so many flavors, including chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry. It is especially enjoyable on a hot day or after a meal.

Facts About Ice Cream

  • Ice cream is a frozen dessert.
  • Common flavors include chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry.
  • It can be served in a cone, cup, or bowl.
  • Popular toppings include sprinkles, fruit, and chocolate sauce.
