#2: Tacos

Tacos are one of my favorite foods because they are full of flavor. I like how I can choose different fillings and toppings. They can be crunchy or soft, and every taco can taste different.

Facts About Tacos

  • Tacos are a popular food from Mexico.
  • They can be made with soft or crispy tortillas.
  • Common fillings include meat, beans, vegetables, and fish.
  • Tacos are often topped with salsa, onions, or cilantro.
