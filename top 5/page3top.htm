#3: Burgers

Burgers are on my list because they are filling and tasty. I like the combination of a bun, a patty, and toppings. I can also add cheese, pickles, or sauces.

Facts About Burgers

  • Burgers are usually served inside a sliced bun.
  • They can be made with beef, chicken, fish, or plant-based patties.
  • Popular toppings include lettuce, tomato, cheese, and pickles.
  • Burgers are commonly served with fries.
