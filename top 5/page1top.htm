#1: Pizza

Pizza is my number favorite food because it is delicious. I like that there are many toppings to choose from. Pizza is also great to share with friends and family.

Facts About Pizza

  • Pizza is often made with dough, sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  • Modern pizza is strongly associated with Naples, Italy.
  • Popular toppings include pepperoni, mushrooms, and olives.
  • Pizza can be baked in a hot oven.
